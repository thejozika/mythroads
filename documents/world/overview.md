# World

The setting and the things in it: regions, NPCs, monsters, story, and the world objects players interact with.

## Scope

- **Continents / regions** — what divides the map, travel between them, regional themes.
- **NPCs** — King, shopkeepers, quest-givers, the antagonist (our Darkling-equivalent).
- **Monsters** — encounter pool, town guardians, bosses, stat tables.
- **Story / chapters** — main quest structure, chapter goals, end-of-game payoff.
- **World objects** — content for treasure spaces, vending machines, quiz spaces, traps, warps.
- **Lore** — names, factions, world history (only as much as the gameplay needs).

## Dokapon reference

Dokapon Story Mode covers 7 continents across 8 chapters (~20–30 h), with the Darkling as the recurring antagonist who can possess the last-place player. Monsters are reused across continents with stat-scaling. See `../../sources/dokapon-core-loop/notes.md` and `../../sources/dokapon-franchise/notes.md`.
