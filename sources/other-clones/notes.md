# Other Clones, Spiritual Successors, and Indie Attempts

Scope: board-game/RPG hybrids that are NOT Dokapon, Itadaki Street/Fortune Street, Momotaro Dentetsu, Culdcept, 100% Orange Juice, or Mario Party (those are covered by sibling agents). The genre is shockingly under-served outside Japan, so this list mixes direct clones, gacha/mobile entries, party-game cousins, and dice-RPG hybrids that share DNA without being on the board (worth a glance for combat/economy ideas).

---

## Direct spiritual successors

### Billion Road (2019, Switch / 2020 PC)
- **Dev/Pub:** Bandai Namco / Acttil (West)
- **Mechanics:** Up to 4 players race across a map of Japan, rolling dice, buying properties, collecting/levelling 50+ recruitable monsters, fighting bosses that periodically threaten investments. The monster mechanic is essentially Dokapon's "field events" merged with Momotetsu property buying.
- **Similarity to Dokapon:** Very high on structure (turn-based map travel + dice + economy + monsters), lower on the PvP-griefing axis — it leans party rather than relationship-ruining.
- **Status:** Got delisted from Steam ~Sept 2024; licensing issue. Still on Switch in some regions.
- **Links:** https://www.useapotion.com/2020/04/billion-road-review/ , https://delistedgames.com/namcos-billion-road-leaving-steam-and-likely-switch-after-september-30th/

### VIractal: World of Viractalia / "Viractal: Will You Trust Your Party?" (2025/2026, PC)
- **Dev/Pub:** Sting (the Dokapon devs themselves)
- **Mechanics:** Board-game-style RPG with dice-roll movement, deck-building, procedural maps, event/combat encounters. Solo or online co-op (not strictly competitive PvP). Saved-up movement points usable in combat and events.
- **Similarity to Dokapon:** It IS the Dokapon team's other genre-experiment. Drops the PvP "ruin friendships" angle in favor of a co-op procedural roguelite take. Direct architectural inspiration mine.
- **Status:** Steam Early Access launched Sept 2025; 1.0 released January 2026, $24.99, "Very Positive" 89% of 332 reviews.
- **Links:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/2909580/ , https://www.gematsu.com/2025/04/sting-announces-board-game-style-rpg-viractal-for-pc , https://rpgamer.com/2026/01/viractal-will-you-trust-your-party-fully-released-on-steam/

### DOKAPON! Sword of Fury (remaster, 2024 Switch JP / 2025 PC EN)
- **Dev/Pub:** Sting / Compile Heart
- Remaster of the 1998 PS1 original. Listed for completeness — it's the "small/short Dokapon" people forget exists. 1-2 players, faster sessions (configurable weeks), 5x speed mode. Closer to the prototype board-game-RPG before the franchise bloated.
- **Links:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/3077020/DOKAPON_Sword_of_Fury/

## Asian mobile / gacha entries (the genre actually thrived here)

### Disney Magical Dice: The Enchanted Board Game (2016-2018, mobile)
- **Dev:** Netmarble (Korea); Disney IP
- Real-time multiplayer Monopoly/Fortune Street hybrid with three player classes and Disney character roster. Originally launched April 2016, soft-relaunched August 2017, delisted October 2018, servers shut December 2018. A textbook example of "Itadaki-Street-with-IP" failing to retain Western mobile audiences.
- **Similarity:** High to Itadaki, medium to Dokapon (less RPG progression).
- **Links:** https://digitalchumps.com/2017/08/24/disney-magical-dice-enchanted-board-game-goes-live-mobile-via-netmarble/

### Game of Dice (2015 mobile / 2025 Steam)
- **Dev:** JOYCITY (Korea)
- Mobile virtual board game; rolls dice, buys territory, collects tolls, bankrupts opponents. Players pick characters with skills (telekinetic master, contract killer, etc.). 50M+ player claim. Real-time PvP with anime-flavored characters.
- **Similarity:** Very strong to Itadaki/Monopoly; lighter RPG (skills only, no gear/levelling). Steam port December 2025 means it's freshly relevant.
- **Links:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.joycity.god , https://god.joycity.com/en

### Lord of Dice (2017+, mobile, KR)
- **Dev:** NGELGAMES / Kakao Games (publisher)
- Gacha tactical RPG that explicitly markets itself as "board games + RPG." Characters ("Dicers") are trapped in the Tower of Infinity; you bounce around board-like dungeon spots Mario-Party-style each turn. Heavier on collection/gacha than competition.
- **Similarity:** Hybrid; the movement layer is genuinely board-like, but the core loop is gacha-collect-and-battle.
- **Links:** https://toucharcade.com/2018/01/29/lord-of-dice-review/

## Party-game cousins (board mechanic + items, weaker RPG)

### Pummel Party (2018, PC)
- **Dev:** Rebuilt Games (indie)
- 4-8 player Mario-Party-with-blood. Roll dice on a giant board accumulating keys → goblets; spaces have effects; items used to grief; minigames between rounds. Very successful indie (~tens of thousands of reviews).
- **Similarity:** Board-game backbone + sabotage items; minimal RPG progression. Useful reference for indie execution of the board layer with online netcode.
- **Links:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/880940/Pummel_Party/

### Talisman: Digital Edition / Talisman: Digital 5th Edition (2014, 2023+)
- **Dev:** Nomad Games (UK), licensed from Games Workshop
- Direct digital port of the 1983 Talisman board game: race to the center, fantasy character classes (warrior/wizard/thief/ghoul), dice movement, encounter cards, combat, leveling. Up to 6 player local/online.
- **Similarity:** Closest Western equivalent of Dokapon's "fantasy RPG on a board" loop, sans PvP-griefing. Very relevant prior art for combat/encounter/event-card systems.
- **Links:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/908060/Talisman_Digital_5th_Edition/

### Board Game Online (BGO, browser, ongoing)
- HTML browser multiplayer "weird" board game; mix of luck and strategy, references-heavy comedy. Long-tail community game. More of a Munchkin/Warhammer-Quest meme cousin but worth bookmarking for tone.
- **Links:** https://boardgame-online.com/ , https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/BoardGameOnline

## Dice-RPGs WITHOUT the board (combat/economy reference only)

These do not share Dokapon's overworld board, but their dice-driven RPG combat and economy are obvious design references for the combat layer of a Dokapon-like.

- **Dicey Dungeons** (Terry Cavanagh, 2019) — Six character classes, deckbuilder roguelike where every action consumes a dice roll. https://store.steampowered.com/app/861540/
- **Slice & Dice** (Tann, 2021) — Tactical dice-rolling roguelike, party of five, 128 hero classes, 73 monsters. The cleanest "each character is a die" combat model. https://store.steampowered.com/app/1775490/
- **Astrea: Six-Sided Oracles** (Little Leo Games / Akupara, 2023) — "Dicebuilder" roguelike with a dual purification/corruption damage system; six characters, 350+ dice. https://store.steampowered.com/app/1755830/
- **Dice Legacy** (Ravenscourt, 2021) — Dice-as-citizens survival city-builder on a ringworld. Different genre, but the "die faces represent abilities/jobs" mirrors Dokapon's job system thematically. https://store.steampowered.com/app/1229460/
- **Dice Throne Digital** (2025+, Kickstarted) — Digital port of the 1v1 dice-and-card combat board game; new roguelite campaign mode. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3855060/

## Mod / fan scene

The active Dokapon modding community lives at GameBanana (separate hubs for the Wii original and the PC "Connect" port) plus an unofficial Discord that ships a mod installer for Connect. Mods are mostly cosmetic/QoL today; no full conversion or new-map projects of note as of search date.
- https://gamebanana.com/games/21391 (Connect)
- https://gamebanana.com/games/15747 (Wii)

## Failed / stalled / "didn't find"

- No Western Kickstarter clone explicitly branded "Dokapon-like" surfaced in searches (multiple queries: "dokapon clone", "dokapon-like indie", Kickstarter, itch.io). The Western indie scene attacks the dice-RPG axis (Dicey/Slice) and the Mario-Party axis (Pummel) but seemingly nobody is making the full board+RPG+PvP-griefing combo on Steam outside of Sting itself.
- Several Chinese Itadaki/Monopoly-clone mobile titles likely exist but are hard to surface in English search. Worth a deeper Chinese-language pass if needed.

---

## Quick architectural takeaways

1. **The genre's only modern direct heir is by Sting themselves (Viractal).** Western indies aren't competing in this niche.
2. **Korean/Chinese mobile is the active market** (Game of Dice, Lord of Dice, Disney Magical Dice) — gacha-monetized and lighter on RPG.
3. **Dice-RPG combat is a solved problem** (Slice & Dice, Dicey Dungeons, Astrea) — these are the references for the battle layer, not the board layer.
4. **Talisman Digital is the Western precedent** for "fantasy board+RPG" and is still actively updated.
5. **Pummel Party** proves a 4-8 player board-with-items product can succeed on Steam if you nail netcode and minigames.
