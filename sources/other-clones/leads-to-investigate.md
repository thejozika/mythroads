# Leads to investigate later

## High priority

- **VIractal: World of Viractalia** — Sting's own 2025/2026 follow-up. Buy and play. Steam page, dev roadmap, RPGSite Early-Access coverage, RPGamer 1.0 review. This is the single most relevant prior-art piece in the entire research scope and warrants a deep solo-research pass. https://store.steampowered.com/app/2909580/
- **Talisman: Digital 5th Edition** — Closest Western "fantasy board + RPG" precedent that is still being updated. Worth playing to study card-event design and 6-player session pacing. https://store.steampowered.com/app/908060/
- **Billion Road** — Modern Bandai Namco take on Itadaki/Momotetsu with a monster-recruitment layer. Delisted from Steam but Switch copies still exist. The monster mechanic is worth dissecting.

## Medium priority

- **Slice & Dice (Tann)** — "Each party member is a die" is a cleaner abstraction than Dokapon's job system; could inspire the combat-side encoding. Cheap on Steam ($9). https://store.steampowered.com/app/1775490/
- **Astrea: Six-Sided Oracles** — Dicebuilder roguelike with dual damage system; useful for thinking about dice-face customization & upgrades.
- **Game of Dice (JOYCITY)** — Steam port December 2025 means it's playable on PC now. Korean mobile design conventions (real-time PvP toll-collection) are worth studying as a counterpoint to Japanese turn-based.

## Low priority / curiosity

- **Disney Magical Dice post-mortem** — Why did a Netmarble + Disney IP Itadaki-clone fail in 2018? Find the digitalchumps coverage and any post-mortem from Netmarble.
- **Lord of Dice** — Less directly relevant; gacha-heavy. Useful only as an example of how to graft RPG progression on a board.
- **GameBanana Dokapon mod scene** — Worth lurking the unofficial Connect Discord for community-driven design ideas (custom bosses, balance complaints, etc.).
- **Chinese-language Itadaki/Monopoly clones** — Almost certainly more entries exist (e.g. on TapTap, BiliBili-distributed games) that English search doesn't surface. Would need a Chinese-language pass.
