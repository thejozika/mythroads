## Notes

- **Only #10 (Fortune Street / Boom Street, Wii, 2011)** received a full Western console release. Its localization is widely attributed to the Mario co-branding and Nintendo's publishing.
- **#11** had brief worldwide mobile availability before being delisted.
- The series has appeared on Famicom, Super Famicom, PS1, PS2, PSP, DS, mobile, Wii, PS4, and PS Vita — every Square-friendly platform of its era.
- All entries are designed/supervised by **Yuji Horii** (Dragon Quest creator).
- Itadaki Street also exists as a **mini-game inside *Dragon Quest III* remakes**, reinforcing the cross-pollination with Horii's main franchise.
