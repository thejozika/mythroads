# Dokapon Kingdom — Full Class List with Stat Growth

All numbers below are sourced primarily from the SuperCheats KainVermillion class guide and corroborated by the Dokapon Fandom Wiki and the Steam "Minmaxing" guide. "Per-Level" = stat gain when the character levels up *while in this job*. Mastery bonus = permanent +stat/level forever after Lv.6.

## Starter Jobs (selectable at character creation)

### Warrior
- **Starting stats**: AT 5 / DF 4 / MG 1 / SP 2 / HP 50
- **Per-level growth**: AT +2, DF +1, HP +10
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 AT per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 7
- **Inventory**: 6 item / 4 field magic
- **Identity**: weapon-and-armor brawler; high baseline AT.

### Magician
- **Starting stats**: AT 5 / DF 2 / MG 3 / SP 3 / HP 40
- **Per-level growth**: MG +2, SP +1, HP +10
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 MG per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 7
- **Inventory**: 6 item / 10 field magic
- **Identity**: nukes via Magic command + heavy field-magic harassment.

### Thief
- **Starting stats**: AT 5 / DF 3 / MG 1 / SP 4 / HP 40
- **Per-level growth**: AT +1, SP +2, HP +10
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 SP per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 7
- **Inventory**: 8 item / 6 field magic
- **Identity**: speed and mobility; Steal field skill; consensus best starter.

## Advanced Jobs (require mastery / item)

### Cleric
- **Unlock**: Master any one of Warrior, Magician, or Thief (Acrobat also qualifies per some sources).
- **Per-level growth**: DF +1, MG +1, HP +20
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 DF per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 7
- **Inventory**: 6 item / 8 field magic
- **Identity**: tank/support, heals.

### Spellsword
- **Unlock**: Master Warrior **and** Magician.
- **Per-level growth**: AT +2, MG +2
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 AT per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 8
- **Inventory**: 8 item / 8 field magic
- **Identity**: hybrid bruiser, scales both Strike and Magic.

### Alchemist
- **Unlock**: Master Magician **and** Thief.
- **Per-level growth**: DF +1, MG +2, SP +1
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 MG per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 8
- **Inventory**: 6 item / 10 field magic
- **Identity**: gold snowball; "Alchemy" battle skill turns damage into gold; "Debug" can drop opponent HP to 1; field skill "Duplicate" copies a random inventory item.

### Ninja
- **Unlock**: Master Warrior **and** Thief.
- **Per-level growth**: AT +2, SP +2
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 SP per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 8
- **Inventory**: 10 item / 6 field magic
- **Identity**: speed/Strike specialist; fastest game-clear; SP harassment.

### Monk
- **Unlock**: Master Cleric (some sources: master Warrior + Thief instead — varies by edition; Connect/Wii confirm Cleric route).
- **Per-level growth**: AT +1, DF +1, HP +20
- **Mastery bonus**: +10 HP per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 8
- **Inventory**: 8 item / 6 field magic
- **Identity**: HP-tank; "Soul Fire" battle skill ramps damage and is the standout skill in the meta.

### Acrobat
- **Unlock**: Obtain **Show Ticket** from Casino Cave (no mastery prereq).
- **Per-level growth**: AT +1, DF +1, SP +1, HP +10
- **Mastery bonus**: +10 HP per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 7
- **Inventory**: 6 item / 6 field magic
- **Identity**: low combat growth, but "best passive in the game" — deception/dodge utility.

### Robo Knight
- **Unlock**: Master **Magician, Thief, Alchemist, Cleric, Monk** (5 jobs); obtain **Lost Technology** from Underwater Shrine and give it to the King.
- **Per-level growth**: AT +1, DF +2, SP +1
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 DF per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 10
- **Inventory**: 10 item / 8 field magic
- **Identity**: late-game tank; DF focus is the trade-off (DF is the weakest stat).

### Hero
- **Unlock**: Master **Warrior, Magician, Thief, Spellsword, Ninja, Acrobat** (6 jobs); obtain **Hero License** from Castle in the Clouds.
- **Per-level growth**: AT +1, DF +1, MG +1, SP +1, **plus +1 to a random stat**
- **Mastery bonus**: +1 AT per level
- **Battles per job-level**: 10
- **Inventory**: 12 item / 12 field magic (largest in game)
- **Identity**: highest base salary; strongest balanced growth; usually unlocks too late to matter competitively.

## Special / Non-Selectable

### Darkling (Kingdom)
- **Not chosen at the castle.** Trigger: Dark Mark from spending ~2 weeks in last place, then land on the Dark Space in Asiana and pay Weber **all assets** (towns, items, magic, gold).
- **Cannot level up; cannot be mastered; no weekly salary.**
- **Duration**: 14 days.
- **Stats**: base parameters tripled; equipped with unique S-tier Darkling weapon/shield/accessory.
- **Skills**: Dark Arts kit; **Castle Panic** (steal a castle, the only asset-growth available to a Darkling); **auto-Counter** vs Strike; can summon Town Bosses to other players' towns; healed and cured of all status (incl. Z-Plague, Doom) on Town Boss tiles; immune to Fear blocking battles.
