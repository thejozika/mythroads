## Key takeaways

- **16+ entries over 32 years**, but only **5 distinct titles localized into English** (Monster Hunter GBA, Kingdom Wii, Journey DS, Kingdom Connect, Sword of Fury PC).
- **Three developer eras**: Asmik Ace solo (1993-2003), handoff (2004), Sting Entertainment owns the IP (2004-present).
- **Two long gaps**: 2011-2020 (almost a decade dormant on consoles) and 2009-2023 (14 years with no English release).
- **Western publishing partners** rotated: Ubi Soft (2001 GBA), Atlus USA (2008-2009 Wii/DS), Bigben (2010 EU Wii), Idea Factory International (2023+).
- **Core multiplayer board-RPG identity is intact** from 1993 to 2023; spinoffs (Gaiden, Monster Hunter, Quest!) consistently underperformed and the series always returns to the 2-4 player board format.
