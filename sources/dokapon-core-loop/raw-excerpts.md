# Dokapon Kingdom — Raw Excerpts

Rulebook-quality passages quoted from sources, with the URL above each excerpt. Useful when designing the core loop directly.

Source: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3032333450 (The Missing Manual — Dokapon Kingdom: Connect)

> "When everyone has taken their turn, one day passes. Weeks are seven days long."

> Win condition: in Story and Normal modes, players must achieve "the greatest Net Worth at the end of the number of weeks" designated for that playthrough. Other modes specify custom objectives at game start.

> "Players 'own' towns by defeating occupying bosses. Town ownership generates daily taxes collectible at bank spaces. Owners can invest to increase daily taxes and net worth. Castles function similarly but scale value based on liberated towns on their continent and generate gems as special local items."

> Battle: "Attack is a basic attack. Strike is an attack with incredible damage … but is countered by Counter. Defense blocks physical attacks; Counter counters strikes specifically; magic defense handles offensive spells. Speed significantly affects strike damage."

> Death: "Upon death, players wait 1-3 turns depending on which entity appears (Cherubs, black-haired angels, or Grim Reaper), losing varying amounts of money, items, or even towns."

Source: https://gamepretty.com/dokapon-kingdom-connect-begginers-manual/ (Beginner's Manual)

> "When a new week starts, a weekly event might happen." Stores close on Sundays. Item gold values multiply by weeks elapsed.

> Net Worth composition: "Towns and castles constitute the majority of net worth, with local items giftable to the king for significantly higher net worth than selling prices."

> Jobs grant "a weekly salary which increases with your job level" plus 3x bonus for completing job-specific goals. Job levels advance per six battles fought.

> "Castles are granted by the king if you do favors for him in Story Mode."

Source: https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/945683-dokapon-kingdom/46932554 (Tips for a beginner — quoted in search results)

> "Towns are the lifeblood of Dokapon's gameplay loop. If you liberate a town by killing the boss that occupies it, you 'own' it. The town will generate taxes every day which you can collect at bank and collection spaces (the blue spaces with a big G on 'em). A town's owner can invest in the town, raising its daily taxes and net worth, from the town itself or from bank spaces."

> "Battles in Dokapon follow a simple formula. Attack is a basic attack. Strike is an attack with incredible damage, usually killing the opponent in a single hit, but is countered by Counter. Defense will defend against any physical attacks (Attack and Strike). Counter will counter-strike if the opponent uses Strike, otherwise you take full damage. Your magic defense ability (M. Guard to start) will defend against whatever offensive magic the opponent carries, but you'll take full damage against anything else."

> "Most of the time, when you land on a blank space, or a town for the first time, you'll fight a monster. 'Field' monsters are relatively weak, whereas town monsters are more powerful as 'bosses'."

> "If you land on a poison swamp space you'll get poisoned and you'll enter a battle. The only way to get rid of it is to heal at a Temple/Dokapon Castle or use a Panacea."

> "To open these you need to have a Magic Key. Locked Box Spaces usually provide you with some nice equipment so try to keep at least 1 or 2 Magic Keys on you whenever you can."

Source: https://www.cgmagonline.com/articles/features/editorial-dokapon-kingdom/ (CGMagazine editorial)

> "The players go through a series of quests, liberating towns, fighting boss monsters, and searching the lands for items the king requests."

> Combat: "The attacker can then either attack normally, try a strike for uber damage, cast an attack spell, or try to use a special ability. The defender then has to choose whether to defend, counter, use defensive magic, or just give up."

> PvP: "Players are quite welcome, and in fact encouraged, to attack each other throughout the game" — direct combat, spellcasting, items, and assassination robots; victory rewards include stealing money, gear, or disfiguring opponents with markers.

> Darkling: gains "access to some seriously broken gear" and can "steal towns from other players just by landing on them," roll multiple spinners, deploy traps, and "steal away castles, which are the grand prize."

Source: https://dokapon.fandom.com/wiki/Story_Mode_(Kingdom) (via search excerpt)

> Prologue: "The adventurers are instructed by the king to travel through a small tutorial area with many weapon, shield, and battle magic spaces. … The goal is to reach the exit space at the end which will send the adventurer to Dokapon Castle on the main map."

> Chapter 1: "After completing the prologue, the adventurers will arrive in Dokapon Castle on the continent of Asiana. They are tasked with defeating two big boss monsters. Once they are both defeated, the king will inform the adventurers that Princess Penny's dog, Cash, has become sick from eating a poisoned donut. The adventurers must find the wise grandma … She will then give them the Antidote item which must then be brought to Dokapon castle. This will cure Cash's illness and the adventurer who brought it back will be rewarded with Khan's Castle."

> Chapter 2: "The adventurers will now be able to go to the second continent, Hallstatt. They must defeat two big boss monsters … Princess Penny's Piggy Bank was stolen by Rico Jr., the prince of the underworld. Whoever brings Princess Penny back to the castle will be rewarded with Cusco Castle."

> Chapter 5: "They must defeat four big boss monsters on this continent, one in each town … the Royal Ring that proves the king's status as the ruler of Dokapon has been stolen by a random monster."

Source: https://dokapon.fandom.com/wiki/Darkling_(Kingdom) (via search excerpts)

> Trigger: "The Dark Mark appears when a character is in last place for an extended period and makes them eligible for the Darkling transformation. To become the Darkling, the adventurer needs to land on the Dark Space in Asiana, where Weber asks for all their Towns, Items, Field Magic, and Cash in exchange for the power of the Darkling, which lasts for 14 days."

> Stats: "In combat, the Darkling possesses very high stats, with their base parameters tripled and given the most powerful equipments in the game, allowing them to easily defeat most players."

> Town summoning: "Darklings can summon Town Bosses to towns ruled by other players by landing on them. … Landing on a town with a monster fully heals the Darkling and cures their status effects, including Z-Plague and Doom."

Source: https://dokapon.fandom.com/wiki/Give_Up (via search excerpt)

> "Give Up, also known as Surrender, is a Battle Command able to be used when defending against an opponent. When used, the character withdraws from the battle and is put on time out for one turn, during which they can't do anything, but also can't be fought or be targeted by Field Magic. It's not possible to give up when the character is Wanted, during the Arena event or against certain enemies (Darkling, assassin, etc.)"

Source: https://dokapon.fandom.com/wiki/Death (via search excerpt)

> Cherubs: "wait for 1 turn, and lose an item or a quarter of your money."
> Black-haired angels: "wait for 2 turns, and will lose half of your money, a few items, or get pranked."
> Grim Reaper: "wait for 3 turns, and may lose all of your money, all items, a piece of equipment or a Town."

Source: https://dokapon.fandom.com/wiki/Loot_Space (via search excerpt)

> "A Loot Space is a Space that starts a roulette that typically gives out an item or piece of equipment to an Adventurer."

> "Loot Spaces are safe from Battle and are closed only when a Darkling uses Banker Strike or Day Off. Depending on the area and type of Loot Space landed on, a different table will be used to determine the possible items and their frequency. A roulette will contain 6, 8, or 10 possible options, again dependent on the area."

Source: https://dokapon.fandom.com/wiki/Field_Magic_(Kingdom) (via search excerpt)

> "Field Magic is a one-time use spell that can be used on either opponents or Monsters. They are most commonly obtained from Magic Spaces and Magic Stores, but they can also be received by attacking the Magic Store or the Merchant, White Loot Spaces, Red Loot Spaces and Locked Boxes."

> Categories: "Damaging Magic containing Fire-type, Lightning-type, Ice-type and Bomb-type Magic; Status Effect Magic containing Weaken-type and Alter-type Magic; Special Magic containing Summon-type, Move-type, and Special-type Magic."

> "Curse inflicts Curse on the opponent, causing them to sometimes attack themselves when they select Attack in a battle. Additionally, Curse All will attempt to Curse all players on the same map as the user, each hit on a player is independent of one another."
