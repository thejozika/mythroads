# Dokapon Kingdom — Iconic / Build-Defining Items

A curated list of the items most cited in community tier lists, item-of-the-week threads, and "broken combos" discussions. Categorized by slot.

## Accessories (Worn)

| Item | Effect | Why Iconic |
|---|---|---|
| **Dokapon Ring** | Boosts all stats; signature accessory of the franchise | Recurring series mascot item; instant power spike |
| **Acro Ring** | Acrobat-themed accessory tied to dodge / Play Dead synergies | Build-enabler for Acrobat / dodge-tank classes |
| **Devil Backup** | Auto-revives or rescues holder on death/curse | Anti-griefing safety net; turns a kill into a swing |
| **Deathblock** | Auto-cancels any attack that would kill the holder | The S-tier defensive accessory; one-shot safety |
| **Revival** | After death, restores 50% HP and clears all status / debuffs | Works in battle AND on world map; "free continue" |
| **Angel Choker** | Equippable revival pendant (Revival in accessory form) | Stack with Revival for redundant lives |
| **Power / Magic / Speed / Defense Rings** | Flat single-stat boosts | Vanilla but always relevant |
| **Fire / Ice / Thunder Bracelets** | Halve damage from matching elemental magic | Hard-counter to mage opponents |

## Field Items / Consumables (Bag)

| Item | Effect | Why Iconic |
|---|---|---|
| **Vanish** | Cancels one negative event/death penalty; saves your gold and gear | Keystone defensive consumable; "3 Vanishes blanks a whole Darkling reign" |
| **Trap** | Placeable on any board space; damages or statuses the next player to land | The defining griefing object; entire spaces become minefields |
| **Spinner** | Guarantees you land on the next picked-space target | Casino farming + targeted town landings |
| **Crystal (4/5)** | Forces a roll of exactly 4 or 5 | Movement control to dodge traps or hit a town |
| **Wings** | Fly to any chosen space | Map-warp for jumping to a tax tile or escaping a hunt |
| **Magic Medicine** | Double ALL stats for ~1 week, then HP drops to 1 when it wears off | High-risk berserker mode; stacks with War Cry / job buffs |
| **Sweet Syrup** | Sticky-status field item that immobilizes/sticks the target | Iconic name; forces a turn skip |
| **Dokapon Ball** | Random major effect (heal, gold, swap, damage, summon) | The series' chaos cube; pure variance |
| **Casino Ticket** | Forces entry to the Casino regardless of map | Late-game money laundering tool |
| **Gold Bug** | Generates gold passively over weeks | Slow but stackable income item |

## Field Magic (Bag, cast on the map)

| Spell | Effect | Why Iconic |
|---|---|---|
| **Come Here** | Teleports ALL other players to the caster's tile (works across maps) | Combo with Speed + Deathblock = forced ambush |
| **Mix-up** | Randomly swaps everyone's positions on the board | Chaos reset; can rescue or grief at random |
| **Mystery** | Random self-effect (heal, full town income, stat buff, etc.) | The "wild card" spell — sometimes wins games on its own |
| **Charm Potion** | Used inside a rival's town; **take ownership of the town** | The premier town-stealing tool (does not work on Castles) |
| **Vacuum** | Drains a target's gold | Direct economic sabotage |
| **Samaritan** | Fully heals a target (self or other) | Support / non-griefing utility |
| **Magic Mirror** | Reflects offensive field magic back at caster | Counter to Come Here / Vacuum / Vanish-bait |

## Class-Skill Sabotage (not items, but item-adjacent)

- **Pickpocket (Thief)** — passing another player on the board steals a random Item or Field Magic.
- **Steal (Thief Lv 2)** — battle skill that takes an item or spell.
- **Attack/Rob a Store (Ninja)** — field skill robs a town's store.
- **Castle Panic** — only mechanism to steal another player's Castle.
- **Darkling form** — last-place-only "evil" job; access to broken gear including **town-snatch on landing**; lasts 2 weeks.
